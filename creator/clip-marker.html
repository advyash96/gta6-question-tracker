    How to use it
    1. Tap Start stream the moment you go live on YouTube.
    2. Started late? Tap Sync time and type the time shown on your stream.
    3. Tap Mark moment or a quick label when something good happens. Fix the name later.
    4. After the stream, copy the text into clips.txt on your PC and run
      python creator/clipper.py stream.mp4 clips.txt --lang hi

    Marks are saved in this browser only. Keep this tab open during the stream.
